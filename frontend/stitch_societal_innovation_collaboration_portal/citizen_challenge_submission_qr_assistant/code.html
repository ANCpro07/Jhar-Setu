What Happens After You Submit?

You receive an instant SMS Tracking ID. Follow the journey as colleges and firms turn your grievance into reality.

1

AI Location & Cluster Validation

Your submission is validated with district geospatial maps, auto-grouped with neighboring requests, and assigned a unique Tracking ID.

2

University Engineering Challenge

Published to state universities (NIT, BIT, RU, BBMKU). Faculty and engineering students choose it as an experiential NEP 2020 credit project.

3

CSR & State Funding Sanction

Corporate CSR partners sponsor the prototype material, manufacturing, and on-site testing costs without burdening the citizen.

4

Village Handover & SMS Closure

The functional unit is deployed in your Panchayat. You and the Gram Pradhan review it before the ticket officially closes.
