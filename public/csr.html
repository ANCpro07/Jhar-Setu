#JH-26108 Renewable Energy / Water Tamar Panchayat, Ranchi District • 8 Verified Citizen Grievances

Defunct 5kW Solar Micro-Grid & Community Borewell System Replacement

The lead-acid storage array has degraded to near-zero capacity, cutting daytime and evening drinking water pumping for 320 tribal households. IIT (ISM) Dhanbad has completed initial feasibility for conversion to an active BMS LiFePO4 battery bank and modular MPPT inverter.

Required CSR Grant ₹3,20,000 Escrow Tranche Model: 3 Stages
school Matched Solver: IIT (ISM) Dhanbad (Dept. of Electrical Eng.)
timer Deployment Timeline: 60 Days
group Beneficiaries: 1,450 Citizens
#JH-26145 Water & Public Health Chiniya Block, Garhwa District • 12 School Submissions

High Fluoride Filtration Columns for 6 Residential Tribal Hostels

Groundwater sources exceed safe limits (measured at 3.4 mg/L vs permissible 1.0 mg/L). BIT Sindri Chemical Engineering wing has designed low-cost activated alumina-bone char adsorption modules requiring local procurement of vessels and initial reagent recharge.

Required CSR Grant ₹4,50,000 Includes 12-month consumable stock
school Matched Solver: BIT Sindri (Dept. of Chemical Engineering)
timer Deployment Timeline: 45 Days
group Beneficiaries: 980 Students
#JH-26190 Forestry & Rural Livelihood Mahuadanr, Latehar District • 4 SHG Clusters

Hygienic Solar Dehydration Unit for Post-Harvest Mahua Flower Processing

Traditional ground drying causes 35% produce loss from dust and animal contamination, severely curtailing market value. Birsa Agricultural University has prototyped an enclosed convection solar dryer capable of handling 500kg daily batch cycles for women-led SHGs.

Required CSR Grant ₹2,80,000 Direct fabrication grant
school Matched Solver: BAU Ranchi (Agri Engineering Division)
timer Deployment Timeline: 30 Days
group Beneficiaries: 110 Forest Dwellers
